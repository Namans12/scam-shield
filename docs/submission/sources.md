## Google logos and trademarks (checked 18 Oct 2026)

Google's partnership guidance says it does not allow its logo to be used by individuals or companies that do business with Google on a limited basis without formal partner status (it could imply endorsement). A 2022 Google Cloud customer co-branding document says every customer may state its relationship to Google Cloud in plain text (for example that it integrates with Google Cloud), may not claim a product is "powered by Google Cloud", and that logo and icon use is limited to specific customer or partner tiers. Sources: https://about.google/brand-resource-center/guidance/partnerships and https://services.google.com/fh/files/misc/external_customer_co_branding_eligibility.pdf . This is not legal advice; the hackathon is run by Google Cloud, which asks entrants to use these products, so the practical risk is low, but plain-text names are the conservative form on a public site. Decision (18 Oct): the website uses plain-text names plus a trademark and non-affiliation note; product icons appear only in the deck architecture slides and the video architecture scene, each with a footnote.
